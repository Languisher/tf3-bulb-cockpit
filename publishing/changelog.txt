In-Game Route Display / 游戏线路显示 · 0.1.75dev

Passed, skipped and unserved stations now explicitly paint white in the vehicle map, including origin/terminus centers. Previously the OFF fill restored the line color, making passed stops look illuminated on yellow routes. Remaining served stops stay yellow; only the next stop optionally blinks. Static directory diagrams keep their normal BULB palette. No added drawing layers, timers or queries.

动态线路图中已过、跳过及本车不经过的站明确显示白色，包含起终点中心；修复黄色线路熄灯后仍沿用线路底色的问题。未到且会经过的站保持常黄，仅下一站可选闪烁。静态目录图保留正常 BULB 配色；不新增图层、计时器或查询。

51 Lua tests and 70 syntax checks passed, including actual native marker fills, arrival/skipping, merged branches and reversal. In-game appearance requires a restart and visual check. / 51 项 Lua 回归及 70 项语法检查通过，包含实际填色、到站／跳站、合并分支及反向行程；实际画面需重启后核对。
